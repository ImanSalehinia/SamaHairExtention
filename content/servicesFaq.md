  - category: Hair Extension Maintenance & Aftercare
    items:
      - q: How long do hair extensions last?
        a: |-
          The time between maintenance appointments depends on the extension method and your natural hair growth. Tape-In extensions typically need repositioning every 6–8 weeks, while Micro / Nano Ring extensions generally need maintenance every 8–10 weeks.

          The lifespan of the hair itself depends on its quality, how often it's worn and how well it's cared for.
      - q: Will hair extensions damage my natural hair?
        a: 'Hair extensions can cause breakage or stress if they are unsuitable for your hair, applied incorrectly or poorly maintained. That''s why we assess your natural hair before recommending a method and provide personalised aftercare advice to help reduce unnecessary tension and damage.'
      - q: How do I look after my hair extensions?
        a: |-
          Proper aftercare helps keep your extensions looking beautiful for longer. We provide personalised aftercare guidance with your appointment.

          Some important tips include:
          - Brush gently, starting from the ends and working upwards.
          - Use suitable hair extension products.
          - Avoid applying oils or conditioner directly to attachment points.
          - Dry your hair and attachment areas properly after washing.
          - Avoid sleeping with wet hair.
          - Follow the aftercare instructions for your chosen method.
      - q: 'Can I wash, blow-dry and heat-style my extensions?'
        a: 'Yes. Human hair extensions can generally be washed, blow-dried and styled with heat. However, gentle handling and suitable products are important. Always use heat protection and follow the aftercare advice for your extension method.'
      - q: Can I colour my hair extensions?
        a: |-
          Our extensions are available in different shades to help create your desired look, including blonde, brunette and highlighted effects.

          We recommend choosing the correct shade during your consultation rather than chemically lightening the extensions. If you want to create a balayage or highlighted look without bleaching your natural hair, different extension shades can be blended to add colour and dimension.
      - q: 'Can I sleep, swim or exercise with hair extensions?'
        a: |-
          This depends on your extension method. Clip-In extensions should be removed before sleeping, showering or swimming.

          For permanent methods, follow the personalised guidance we provide on sleeping, exercise and water exposure.
