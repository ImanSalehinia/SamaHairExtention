sections:
  - title: What Are Micro / Nano Ring Extensions?
    img: ""
    body: >
      Micro and Nano Ring extensions use individual strands of premium human
      hair, attached to your natural hair using small rings or beads.


      The extensions are fitted strand by strand, without the use of glue or heat during application. Nano Rings use smaller attachment rings than Micro Rings, offering a more discreet fitting option.


      Both methods are designed to add length and volume while allowing natural-looking movement and blending.
  - title: The Application Process
    img: ""
    body: >
      We begin by sectioning your clean, dry hair into neat, even sections.
      Small sections of your natural hair are then threaded through the rings
      alongside the extension strands.


      The rings are carefully positioned and secured using a specialist tool, creating individual attachments without adhesive or heat during application.


      Each strand is placed with attention to detail to achieve an even, comfortable and natural-looking result.
  - title: Who Are Micro / Nano Ring Extensions Best For?
    img: ""
    body: >
      Micro and Nano Ring extensions are popular with clients who prefer
      individual strand application without glue or heat during fitting.


      They are suitable for adding length and volume, with the choice of Micro or Nano Rings depending on your natural hair, desired result and the level of discretion you need.


      During your consultation, we'll assess your natural hair and help you choose the most suitable method.
  - title: Aftercare & Maintenance
    img: ""
    body: >
      Micro and Nano Ring extensions need regular maintenance as your natural
      hair grows and the rings move away from the roots.


      For better maintenance:

      - Brush gently, starting from the ends and working upwards.

      - Hold the roots when brushing to avoid putting tension on the attachments.

      - Avoid pulling or twisting the extension strands.

      - Use suitable hair extension aftercare products.

      - Follow your personalised aftercare advice.
