  - title: What Are Tape-In Extensions?
    img: ""
    body: >
      Tape-In extensions use ultra-thin, pre-taped hair panels that are attached
      to small sections of your natural hair using adhesive tape. The extensions
      lie flat against your head, creating a lightweight and discreet finish
      that blends beautifully with your own hair.


      They are a popular choice for adding length and volume with a smooth, natural-looking result.
  - title: The Application Process
    img: ""
    body: >
      We begin by sectioning your clean, dry hair into neat, even rows. A small
      section of your natural hair is then placed between two adhesive tape
      panels to secure the extensions in position.


      The application requires no heat during fitting and is carried out carefully to ensure comfortable placement and an even, natural-looking blend.
  - title: Who Are Tape-In Extensions Best For?
    img: ""
    body: >
      Tape-In extensions are a popular option for clients who want to add
      noticeable length and volume with lightweight, flat attachments.


      They can be suitable for a variety of hair types. During your consultation, we'll assess your natural hair, discuss your desired result and help you decide whether this method is right for you.
  - title: Aftercare & Maintenance
    img: ""
    body: >
      Tape-In extensions typically need to be repositioned every 6–8 weeks,
      depending on your natural hair growth and individual needs.


      For better maintenance:

      - Avoid applying oils or conditioner directly to the tape attachments.

      - Brush gently, starting from the ends and working upwards.

      - Use suitable hair extension aftercare products.

      - Follow your personalised aftercare advice.
