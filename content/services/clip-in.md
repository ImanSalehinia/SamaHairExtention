  - title: What Are Clip-In Extensions?
    img: ""
    body: >
      Clip-In extensions are temporary hair extensions designed to add instant
      length and volume without a permanent attachment.


      They come in sections with small clips that attach securely to your natural hair and can be easily removed whenever you like.


      A flexible option for changing your look without committing to a long-term extension method.
  - title: The Application Process
    img: ""
    body: >
      Your natural hair is sectioned into neat rows, creating a base for each
      extension piece.


      The clips are carefully attached close to the roots and blended with your natural hair to create a fuller, more seamless finish.


      The application is quick and does not require glue, heat or chemical products. The extensions can be removed at the end of the day.
  - title: Who Are Clip-In Extensions Best For?
    img: ""
    body: >
      Clip-In extensions are ideal for anyone who wants extra length or volume
      without wearing extensions permanently.


      They are particularly suitable for:

      - Special occasions, parties and events.

      - Adding volume for everyday styling.

      - Trying a longer hairstyle before choosing a permanent method.

      - Clients who prefer to put in and remove their extensions themselves.


      During your consultation, we'll help you choose the right length, shade and volume for your desired look.
  - title: Aftercare & Maintenance
    img: ""
    body: >
      Clip-In extensions are easy to maintain and should be removed before
      sleeping, showering or swimming.


      For better care:

      - Remove the extensions gently after each use.

      - Brush carefully, starting from the ends and working upwards.

      - Store them neatly in a clean, dry place.

      - Avoid excessive heat styling.

      - Wash only when needed, using suitable hair extension products.
