    img: ""
    body: |
      Keratin Bond extensions require proper care and professional maintenance to keep them looking their best.

      As your natural hair grows, the bonds gradually move away from the roots and eventually need professional removal and replacement.

      To look after your extensions:
      - Brush gently, starting from the ends and working upwards.
      - Avoid pulling or putting unnecessary tension on the bonds.
      - Keep conditioner and heavy oils away from the bonds.
      - Use suitable hair extension aftercare products.
