## Start With Your Natural Hair

The right extension method always starts with an honest look at your natural hair — its thickness, density and how it grows. Fine or fragile hair generally suits lighter, more discreet attachments, while thicker hair can usually carry a wider range of methods comfortably.

This is exactly why every appointment at Sama Hair Extensions begins with a free consultation. We assess your hair in person before recommending a method, rather than guessing from a photo or a quick chat.

## Think About Your Lifestyle

Some methods are built for low maintenance and flexibility, others for a longer-term, more seamless result.

A quick way to narrow it down:

- Want extra length or volume only for special occasions? Clip-In extensions give you that without any commitment.
- Want a long-term, natural-looking transformation with strand-by-strand placement? Keratin Bond is a popular choice.
- Prefer no heat or glue during application? Micro Ring or Nano Ring extensions may suit you better.
- Want a quick application with a flat, seamless finish? Tape-In extensions are usually the fastest route to noticeably longer, fuller hair.
