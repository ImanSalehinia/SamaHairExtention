If you are looking for a way to add length and volume to your natural hair, tape in hair extensions may be an option worth considering.

Known for their flat attachment points and relatively quick application, tape extensions are popular with people who want a fuller hairstyle.

But how do they work, how long do they last and are they right for you?

Here is everything you need to know before booking your appointment.

## What Are Tape In Hair Extensions?

Tape in extensions are hair panels attached to your natural hair using specially designed adhesive tapes.

A small section of your natural hair is placed between two extension panels, creating a secure attachment.

Because the panels lie relatively flat against the head, they can create a smooth finish when correctly installed.

## Benefits of Tape In Hair Extensions

### Natural-Looking Results

When the colour, length and placement are selected correctly, tape extensions can blend beautifully with natural hair.

### Flat Attachment Points

The flat design can make the extensions comfortable and relatively discreet.

### Adding Volume and Length

Tape extensions can help create fuller-looking hair and add noticeable length.

### Relatively Quick Application

Compared with some strand-by-strand methods, tape extensions can be quicker to install.

### Different Styling Options

Human hair tape extensions can generally be styled in different ways, subject to their quality and care instructions.

## How Long Do Tape In Hair Extensions Last?

Tape extensions generally need professional maintenance around every 6–8 weeks.

The extension hair itself may last considerably longer, depending on quality and aftercare.

Some high-quality sets can be reused, but this depends on their condition and the manufacturer's guidance.

## Are Tape In Extensions Suitable for Thin Hair?

They may be suitable for some people with fine hair.

However, the attachment panels need sufficient natural hair to support their weight.

A professional should assess your hair density and condition before recommending tape extensions.

## How Are Tape Extensions Applied?

The process generally involves:

- Assessing the natural hair.
- Selecting a suitable extension colour and length.
- Preparing and sectioning the hair.
- Placing the tape extensions.
- Checking the placement and distribution.
- Blending the extensions with the natural hair.

The exact process depends on the extension system being used.

## How to Care for Tape In Extensions

- Brush gently using a suitable brush.
- Avoid applying conditioner to the tape attachments.
- Avoid heavy oils around the adhesive.
- Dry the roots and attachment areas thoroughly.
- Avoid excessive heat near the tapes.
- Sleep with completely dry hair.
- Attend maintenance appointments on time.

## How Are Tape In Extensions Removed?

Tape extensions should be removed using an appropriate professional removal product.

The adhesive needs to be carefully dissolved before the panels are separated from the natural hair.

Never pull the panels apart or attempt to remove them by force.

## Are Tape In Extensions Worth It?

If you want a flat attachment and fuller-looking hair, tape extensions may be worth considering.

However, they are not ideal for every hair type or lifestyle.

A professional assessment can help you decide whether they are suitable.
