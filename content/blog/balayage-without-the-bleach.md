## How It Works

Rather than lightening your natural hair with bleach, different extension shades can be blended in to add colour, dimension and movement — creating a balayage or highlighted effect while your own hair stays untouched.

## Why Clients Choose This Option

This approach is popular with clients who want a noticeable colour change without the commitment, damage risk or ongoing root maintenance that comes with chemical lightening.

## Getting the Shade Right

The key to a natural result is choosing the correct extension shade during your consultation, rather than trying to lighten the extensions themselves afterwards. We’ll help you select and blend shades that complement your natural colour for a seamless finish.
