## Assessing Your Natural Hair

Every consultation starts with a close look at your natural hair — its thickness, density, condition and how it grows. This is what determines which extension methods will actually suit you, rather than just which ones you like the look of.

## Talking Through Your Goals

We’ll talk through what you want to achieve — extra length, more volume, a colour change, or a bit of everything — and how that fits with your daily routine and styling habits.

## Colour Matching & Next Steps

If you go ahead, we’ll colour match your hair to find the right shade or shade blend, confirm the amount of hair needed and talk through pricing before booking your fitting appointment.

Just bring along:

- Yourself, with your hair in its usual condition (no need to wash or style beforehand).
- A clear reference photo in natural light, if you’re after a specific colour or look.
- Any questions about maintenance, pricing or timing — nothing is too small to ask.
