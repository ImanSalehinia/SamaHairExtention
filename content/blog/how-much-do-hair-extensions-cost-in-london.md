If you are thinking about getting hair extensions, one of the first questions you probably have is: how much will they cost?

The price of hair extensions in London can vary considerably depending on the installation method, hair quality, length, quantity and maintenance requirements.

Whether you are considering nano rings, tape ins or another method, understanding the full cost can help you make a better decision.

In this guide, we explain what affects hair extension prices and what you should consider before booking an appointment.

## How Much Do Hair Extensions Cost in London?

There is no single price for all hair extension services.

The final cost depends on several factors, including:

- The extension method.
- The quality of the human hair.
- The length of the extensions.
- The amount of hair required.
- Whether you need a partial or full-head application.
- Installation and blending time.
- Future maintenance and removal.

For example, someone looking for subtle volume will usually need a different amount of hair from someone wanting a dramatic, long transformation.

## What Affects the Price of Hair Extensions?

### Hair Quality

Hair quality is one of the biggest factors affecting the total cost.

Human hair extensions are generally more expensive than synthetic alternatives.

Remy human hair is valued because the hair cuticles are aligned in the same direction, which can help reduce tangling when the hair is properly processed and maintained.

However, the term Remy alone does not guarantee quality. The source, processing, construction and condition of the hair also matter.

### Extension Length

Longer extensions generally require more material and may cost more.

For example, 18-inch extensions and 24-inch extensions are not necessarily priced the same.

Longer hair may also require more careful blending to achieve a natural finish.

### Amount of Hair Required

Some clients only want extra volume, while others want both length and thickness.

The quantity required depends on your natural hair density and the look you want to achieve.

Adding more hair is not always the best solution, particularly if your natural hair is fine or fragile.

### Application Method

Different extension methods involve different materials, application times and maintenance requirements.

Nano rings, micro rings, tape ins and keratin bond extensions all have different installation processes.

The right method should be selected according to your natural hair and lifestyle, rather than price alone.

## What About Hair Extension Maintenance Costs?

The initial application is only part of the overall cost.

Semi-permanent extensions need regular professional maintenance.

Depending on the method, this may include:

- Moving up or repositioning extensions.
- Removing and replacing attachment points.
- Checking the condition of the extension hair.
- Detangling and separating natural hair.
- Replacing damaged or worn extension hair.

Maintenance intervals vary by method and individual hair growth. Tape extensions often require attention around every 6–8 weeks, while nano ring extensions are commonly maintained around every 8–12 weeks.

Always follow the schedule recommended by your stylist.

## Are Cheap Hair Extensions Worth It?

A lower initial price may seem attractive, but it is important to understand what is included.

Before booking, ask:

- Is the hair included in the quoted price?
- Is the installation included?
- Is blending or cutting included?
- Is removal charged separately?
- How much does maintenance cost?
- Can the extension hair be reused?

A clear price breakdown helps you avoid unexpected costs.

## How Can You Get the Best Value from Hair Extensions?

Here are some practical tips:

- Choose the right method — a method that suits your hair may require less correction and provide a more manageable result.
- Invest in suitable hair quality — the quality of the extension hair affects how it looks and behaves over time.
- Follow aftercare advice — proper brushing, washing and styling can help maintain the condition of your extensions.
- Keep up with maintenance — delaying appointments can lead to tangling and additional repair costs.
- Ask for a personalised quote — your price should reflect your hair and desired result.
