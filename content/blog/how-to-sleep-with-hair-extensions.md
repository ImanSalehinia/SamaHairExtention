Getting a good night's sleep should not mean waking up with tangled hair.

If you wear hair extensions, your nighttime routine is an important part of keeping them in good condition.

Friction between your hair and pillow can contribute to knots, dryness and tangling, particularly around extension attachment points.

Here are some simple ways to protect your extensions while sleeping.

## Never Sleep with Wet Hair Extensions

One of the most important rules is to make sure your hair is completely dry before going to bed.

Wet hair is more vulnerable to tangling, and damp attachment areas can become difficult to manage.

After washing, gently towel-dry your hair and make sure the roots and attachments are thoroughly dry.

## Brush Your Hair Before Bed

Before sleeping, gently brush your extensions.

Start at the ends and work upwards.

Hold the hair near the attachment points to avoid unnecessary pulling.

Make sure you gently separate the hair around your roots.

## Try a Loose Braid

A loose braid can help keep longer extensions together during the night.

Avoid tight braids or ponytails that create tension on your natural hair.

The hairstyle should feel comfortable and should not pull on the attachments.

## Use a Silk or Satin Pillowcase

Cotton pillowcases can create friction as you move during sleep.

Silk or satin pillowcases may help reduce friction and make your hair easier to manage in the morning.

They are not a substitute for proper brushing and drying, but they can be a useful addition to your routine.

## Avoid Sleeping with Tight Hairstyles

Tight buns, high ponytails and restrictive hairstyles can place unnecessary tension on extension attachments.

Choose a relaxed style that keeps the hair secure without pulling.

## How to Sleep with Nano Ring Extensions

For nano ring extensions:

- Brush gently before bed.
- Make sure the roots are dry.
- Keep the hair loosely secured if needed.
- Avoid pulling or twisting the attachment points.
- Check for tangles underneath the hair.

## How to Sleep with Tape In Extensions

For tape extensions:

- Ensure the adhesive areas are completely dry.
- Avoid tight hairstyles.
- Keep heavy styling products away from the tape attachments.
- Use a gentle brushing routine.
- Follow your specialist's instructions.

## What Should You Do If Your Extensions Are Tangled in the Morning?

Do not pull a brush through a knot.

Hold the hair above the tangle and carefully separate it with your fingers.

Use an appropriate extension brush and work gradually from the ends.

If the tangling is close to the roots or attachment points, ask your stylist for help.

## Create a Simple Nighttime Routine

Your nighttime routine does not need to be complicated.

- Brush gently.
- Check that your hair is dry.
- Separate any small tangles.
- Secure your hair loosely.
- Use a satin or silk pillowcase if you prefer.
