Hair extensions can transform your hairstyle, but eventually they need to be removed, replaced or repositioned.

Whether you wear nano rings, tape extensions or another method, correct removal is essential.

Removing extensions incorrectly can cause unnecessary pulling, breakage and damage to your natural hair.

In this guide, we explain how professional hair extension removal works and what you should know before your appointment.

## Why Is Professional Hair Extension Removal Important?

Hair extensions are attached to your natural hair using specific attachment systems.

These attachments need to be released using the correct tools and techniques.

Pulling or cutting extensions without understanding the attachment method can damage your natural hair.

Professional removal helps reduce unnecessary tension and makes it easier to assess your hair condition.

## How Are Nano Ring Extensions Removed?

Nano ring extensions are generally removed using specialist pliers.

The stylist carefully opens each ring and releases the extension strand.

The natural hair is then gently separated and detangled.

Depending on the condition of the hair, the extensions may be suitable for reuse.

## How Are Tape In Extensions Removed?

Tape extensions require a suitable adhesive remover.

The removal process generally involves:

- Applying an appropriate remover to the tape.
- Allowing it to work according to product instructions.
- Carefully separating the tape panels.
- Removing adhesive residue as required.
- Gently detangling the natural hair.
- Checking the extension hair and natural hair condition.

Never force tape panels apart.

## Can You Remove Hair Extensions at Home?

Some extension systems are designed for professional removal, and attempting to remove them yourself can be risky.

You may accidentally pull natural hair, break strands or damage attachment points.

For professional semi-permanent extensions, removal by an experienced specialist is generally the safer option.

## What Happens to Your Natural Hair After Removal?

It is normal to notice shed hairs after removing extensions.

Natural hair sheds regularly, but some shed hairs may have remained trapped around attachment points while the extensions were installed.

However, excessive breakage, thinning or scalp irritation should not be dismissed as normal.

If you notice unusual hair loss, seek advice from an appropriate healthcare professional.

## Can Hair Extensions Be Reinstalled After Removal?

Some extension hair can be reused.

This depends on:

- Original hair quality.
- Condition of the hair.
- Amount of shedding.
- Tangling.
- Dryness.
- Attachment system.
- Manufacturer recommendations.

The extension hair should be inspected before reinstallation.

## How Much Does Hair Extension Removal Cost?

Removal prices vary depending on:

- Extension method.
- Number of attachment points.
- Amount of hair.
- Time required.
- Whether detangling is needed.
- Whether new extensions are being installed.

Always confirm the price before booking.

## What Should You Do After Removing Hair Extensions?

After removal:

- Gently brush your natural hair.
- Use suitable shampoo and conditioner.
- Avoid excessive heat styling.
- Assess the condition of your hair.
- Allow your hair to rest if needed.
- Seek professional advice before reinstalling extensions if you notice damage.
