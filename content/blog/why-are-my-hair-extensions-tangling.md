Hair extensions should feel manageable and look smooth, but sometimes they become tangled, knotted or difficult to brush.

If your extensions are constantly tangling, it is worth understanding why.

Tangling can happen for several reasons, including dryness, friction, incorrect brushing and delayed maintenance.

Here are the most common causes and how to deal with them.

## 1. Your Hair Extensions Are Too Dry

Extension hair does not receive natural oils from your scalp in the same way as your natural hair.

As a result, the lengths and ends may become dry over time.

Dry hair is more likely to feel rough and develop knots.

What to do:

- Use a suitable moisturising conditioner.
- Apply a lightweight product to the mid-lengths and ends.
- Avoid excessive heat styling.
- Follow the manufacturer's aftercare instructions.

## 2. You Are Not Brushing Correctly

Incorrect brushing can make tangling worse.

Pulling a brush from the roots downwards can put unnecessary stress on attachment points.

Start at the ends and gradually work upwards.

Hold your hair near the attachment area and use gentle movements.

## 3. Your Extensions Are Not Being Maintained on Time

As your natural hair grows, extension attachments move further away from the scalp.

Naturally shed hair can collect around the attachment points.

If maintenance is delayed, this can contribute to matting.

Follow your recommended maintenance schedule.

Do not wait until your hair becomes severely tangled.

## 4. You Are Sleeping with Wet Hair

Wet hair can become tangled more easily, especially when it rubs against a pillow overnight.

Always dry your roots and attachment areas properly before going to bed.

Consider loosely braiding your hair to reduce movement and friction.

## 5. Your Hair Products Are Not Suitable

Heavy products can leave residue, particularly around certain attachment systems.

Some products may also affect adhesive bonds.

Use products recommended for your extension method.

Apply conditioner mainly to the lengths and ends rather than attachment points.

## 6. Your Extensions Are Exposed to Too Much Heat

Frequent heat styling can dry out extension hair and make it more difficult to manage.

Use a suitable heat protectant and moderate temperatures.

Reduce unnecessary straightening and curling.

## 7. The Extension Hair Quality May Be Poor

Not all extension hair is the same.

Poorly processed or damaged hair may become difficult to manage even with a good aftercare routine.

If your extensions repeatedly tangle despite correct care, ask your specialist to assess their condition.

## How to Detangle Hair Extensions Safely

Follow these steps:

- Separate the tangled section.
- Hold the hair above the knot.
- Use your fingers to loosen the tangle.
- Start brushing from the ends.
- Work upwards slowly.
- Avoid pulling near attachment points.

If the matting is close to the roots or rings, do not force it apart.

## When Should You Contact Your Hair Extension Specialist?

Seek professional help if you notice:

- Severe matting.
- Knots around attachment points.
- Persistent pulling.
- Loose extensions.
- Pain or discomfort.
- Unusual breakage.

These issues may require professional maintenance or removal.
