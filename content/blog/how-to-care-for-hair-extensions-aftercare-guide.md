Investing in hair extensions is exciting, but keeping them looking beautiful requires the right care.

Whether you have nano ring, tape in or another type of professional hair extensions, your daily routine can make a significant difference to their appearance and lifespan.

From washing and brushing to sleeping and styling, this guide covers the essential hair extension aftercare tips you should know.

## 1. Brush Your Hair Extensions Correctly

Brushing is one of the most important parts of your aftercare routine.

Extensions can become tangled, especially around the attachment points and underneath the hair.

To reduce tangling:

- Use a suitable extension brush.
- Start brushing from the ends.
- Work upwards gradually.
- Hold your hair near the attachment points.
- Avoid pulling or brushing aggressively.
- Brush gently underneath your hair as well.

Do not force a brush through a knot. Carefully separate it with your fingers first.

## 2. How Often Should You Wash Hair Extensions?

You do not necessarily need to wash your extensions every day.

Washing frequency depends on your scalp, lifestyle, hair products and extension method.

For many people, washing every few days is a reasonable starting point, but you should adjust this to your needs.

When washing:

- Gently detangle your hair before getting it wet.
- Use lukewarm water.
- Apply a suitable shampoo to the scalp.
- Clean the lengths gently without rubbing aggressively.
- Rinse thoroughly.
- Apply conditioner to the mid-lengths and ends.
- Keep conditioner and heavy oils away from attachment points.

Always follow the specific aftercare instructions for your extension method and products.

## 3. Choose the Right Shampoo and Conditioner

Using suitable hair products can help keep extension hair manageable.

Look for products that are appropriate for your extension type.

Many extension specialists recommend avoiding heavy or oily products around attachment points, particularly with tape extensions.

You should also avoid applying conditioner directly to tape bonds or other attachment areas unless your stylist advises otherwise.

Remember that different extension systems can have different product requirements.

## 4. Never Go to Bed with Wet Hair Extensions

Going to bed with wet extensions can increase tangling and make the attachment areas harder to manage.

After washing:

- Gently remove excess water with a towel.
- Avoid rubbing your hair.
- Dry the attachment areas carefully.
- Make sure the roots and attachment points are completely dry.
- Use a low or medium heat setting when blow-drying.

Do not leave damp hair packed tightly against your scalp overnight.

## 5. How to Sleep with Hair Extensions

Your sleeping routine can help reduce tangling.

Before going to bed:

- Make sure your hair is completely dry.
- Brush gently.
- Tie your hair in a loose braid or ponytail if appropriate for your method.
- Consider using a silk or satin pillowcase.

Avoid tight hairstyles that pull on the attachment points.

## 6. Be Careful When Using Heat Styling Tools

Human hair extensions can usually be styled with heated tools, but excessive heat can make the hair dry and reduce its lifespan.

For better results:

- Always use a heat protectant suitable for extensions.
- Use a moderate temperature.
- Avoid direct heat on attachment points.
- Limit unnecessary straightening and curling.
- Allow your hair to cool before brushing out curls.

If your extensions are made from synthetic hair, check the manufacturer's heat recommendations before using styling tools.

## 7. Prevent Tangling and Matting

Tangling can happen when hair extensions are not brushed or dried properly.

To reduce the risk:

- Brush regularly.
- Separate attachment areas gently.
- Avoid sleeping with wet hair.
- Use suitable lightweight conditioning products.
- Avoid excessive friction.
- Attend your maintenance appointments on time.

If you notice matting near the roots, do not try to pull it apart aggressively. Ask your extension specialist for help.

## 8. How Often Do Hair Extensions Need Maintenance?

Maintenance depends on the method and how quickly your natural hair grows.

As a general guide:

- Tape extensions: approximately every 6–8 weeks.
- Nano ring extensions: approximately every 8–12 weeks.

These are typical ranges, not guarantees.

Your stylist may recommend an earlier appointment depending on your hair condition and the position of the attachments.

Never wait until extensions become severely tangled or uncomfortable before arranging maintenance.

## 9. Can You Swim with Hair Extensions?

Swimming is possible with some types of hair extensions, but chlorine and salt water can affect the condition of extension hair.

If you swim:

- Tie your hair loosely to reduce tangling.
- Avoid unnecessary exposure to chlorinated or salt water.
- Rinse your hair with fresh water afterwards.
- Follow your extension specialist's washing instructions.
- Dry your attachment points carefully.

If you swim frequently, discuss this with your stylist before choosing an extension method.

## 10. When Should Hair Extensions Be Removed?

Hair extensions should be removed or repositioned according to their recommended maintenance schedule.

You may need professional attention if you notice:

- Excessive tangling.
- Loose attachment points.
- Uncomfortable pulling.
- Visible or slipping attachments.
- Significant changes in your natural hair condition.

Never pull extensions out yourself.

Professional removal helps protect your natural hair and makes it easier to assess its condition.

## Your Hair Extension Aftercare Checklist

- Brush gently every day.
- Detangle before washing.
- Use suitable shampoo and conditioner.
- Keep heavy products away from attachment points.
- Dry your roots and attachments thoroughly.
- Avoid excessive heat.
- Sleep with dry, gently secured hair.
- Attend maintenance appointments on time.
- Seek professional help if you notice discomfort or matting.
