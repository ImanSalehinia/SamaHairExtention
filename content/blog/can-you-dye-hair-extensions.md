If you wear hair extensions, you may eventually want to change their colour.

Perhaps your natural hair has become lighter, you want a warmer tone or you simply fancy a different look.

But can you dye hair extensions?

The answer depends on the type and quality of the hair.

## Can You Dye Human Hair Extensions?

Some human hair extensions can be coloured, but this does not mean every set is suitable for chemical processing.

The hair may already have undergone treatments during manufacturing.

Further colouring can affect its texture, strength and appearance.

Always check with the manufacturer or your extension specialist before making changes.

## Can You Bleach Hair Extensions?

Bleaching is particularly risky.

It involves removing pigment and can cause dryness, breakage and damage to the hair.

If the extensions have already been processed, bleaching them again may significantly affect their condition.

For this reason, lightening extensions is generally best avoided unless a qualified colour professional confirms that the hair can tolerate the process.

## Can You Dye Synthetic Hair Extensions?

Most conventional synthetic hair extensions cannot be coloured using standard hair dye.

Their artificial fibres do not behave like natural human hair.

Some specialist products may be designed for particular synthetic fibres, but you should always check the manufacturer's instructions.

## Why Colour Matching Is Important

One of the most important parts of achieving natural-looking extensions is choosing the right shade.

Your hair may contain several tones rather than one uniform colour.

Consider:

- Your root colour.
- Mid-length colour.
- Ends.
- Highlights or lowlights.
- Warm or cool undertones.

A professional colour match can help create a seamless result.

## Is It Better to Colour Your Natural Hair or Extensions?

Whenever possible, discuss the colour plan before installing your extensions.

This allows the stylist to select extension hair that complements your intended colour.

Changing the colour of installed extensions may be more complicated and could affect the attachment areas.

## How to Maintain Coloured Human Hair Extensions

If your extension hair has been professionally coloured:

- Use suitable colour-safe products.
- Avoid excessive heat.
- Limit unnecessary washing.
- Condition the lengths and ends.
- Protect hair from prolonged sun exposure.
- Follow professional aftercare advice.

## What Should You Do Before Colouring Extensions?

Before applying any colour:

- Identify whether the hair is human or synthetic.
- Check the manufacturer's colour restrictions.
- Assess the current condition.
- Consult a professional colourist.
- Discuss a strand test where appropriate.

Never assume that because extensions are made from human hair, they can safely undergo every colouring process.
