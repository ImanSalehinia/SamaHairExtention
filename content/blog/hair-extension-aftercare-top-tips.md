## Brushing the Right Way

Always brush gently, starting from the ends and working your way up towards the roots. This reduces tension on the attachment points and helps prevent unnecessary shedding or breakage.

## Washing & Drying

How you wash and dry your extensions matters just as much as how often.

A few key habits to build in:

- Avoid applying oils or conditioner directly to the attachment points.
- Dry your hair and attachment areas properly after washing — never sleep with wet hair.
- Use suitable hair extension aftercare products rather than regular heavy conditioners.
- Follow the specific aftercare instructions for your chosen method, as these can vary.

## Know When to Book Maintenance

As your natural hair grows, attachments gradually move away from the roots and need professional repositioning. Tape-In extensions typically need attention every 6–8 weeks, while Micro and Nano Ring extensions generally last 8–10 weeks between appointments.

Keeping to a regular maintenance schedule is one of the simplest ways to protect both your extensions and your natural hair underneath.
